BTC/USDT Critical Support Level Analysis: Will the 60k psychological barrier hold?

CryptoWhale_KR 2023.10.27 14:32 Views 12,482

As we observe the recent price action in the BTC/USDT pair, it's clear that the market is entering a phase of high volatility. The daily candle recently touched the 200-day Moving Average, signaling a potential trend reversal or a deep correction if support fails to materialize.

"The current accumulation phase reminds me of the Q4 2020 setup. While retail sentiment is fearful, institutional order blocks are being filled at these levels."

The RSI is currently hovering around 35, bordering on oversold territory. Historically, entries in this zone have provided favorable risk-to-reward ratios for swing traders looking for a 15-20% bounce back to the mid-channel resistance.

In conclusion, traders should watch the 59,500 level closely. A 4-hour close below this could trigger a cascade of liquidations towards the 52k region. On the upside, reclaiming 62,800 is essential for any bullish thesis to remain valid.
